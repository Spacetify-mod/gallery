# Starry Night · Orange

Starry Night is one of Spicetify's official themes: a night sky full of stars around the panels. This is its **Orange** colour scheme, on a dark background with a yellow accent.

## What it changes

- **All of Spotify's colours**: its 16 colour sets, so no Spotify green is left anywhere, even in messages, white buttons or menus.
- **The frame too**: the top bar, the player bar and the space between the panels take the theme's colours.
- **Always readable**: the contrast of every text with its background was checked, and adjusted where the original colours were too faint.
- **The starry sky**: stars twinkle in the frame around the panels (they stay still if your computer asks for less motion).

## Made to last

Spicetify's version of this theme changes Spotify's code and generated class names, which break with Spotify's updates. This one only uses Spotify's colour variables and its `data-testid` hooks: it keeps working after updates. `spacetify doctor` checks it on your Spotify.
