# Pop-up Lyrics

The lyrics of the song playing, in a small window that stays on top of your other windows: keep singing while you work or play.

## How to use it

- Click the **lyrics window** button in the player bar. Click it again to close the window.
- The line being sung lights up and the lyrics scroll by themselves. **Click a line** to go there in the song.
- Point at the window to show the **− / +** buttons, if the lyrics are a little early or late.

## Better than the original

A real window, with the blurred cover behind the lyrics, text size and alignment in the settings, and words you can click.

## Where the lyrics come from

From [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), a free and open collection of synced lyrics. Only the title, artist, album and length of the song are sent to it.

## Made to last

It only uses spacetify's API, never Spotify's code: it keeps working after Spotify's updates.
