# No Podcasts

Your home page, with only music: podcasts, episodes and audiobooks are hidden.

## Clean, not broken

- Cards are hidden one by one, and a row left empty is hidden too, so no lonely titles stay behind.
- A banner for one episode in the middle of a row of playlists goes, the playlists stay.
- It recognises podcasts by their links, so it works in every language.

## Settings

Podcasts, audiobooks, or both, on the home page only or everywhere. Nothing is removed: turn it off and everything is back.
