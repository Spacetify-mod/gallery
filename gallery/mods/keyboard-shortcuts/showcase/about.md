# Keyboard Shortcuts

Use Spotify without the mouse.

## The keys

- **Move around:** J and K to scroll, G and Shift + G for the top and the bottom, H and L to go back and forward.
- **Music:** N and P for next and previous, 0 to 9 to jump to 0 %, 10 % … 90 % of the song, Shift + arrows to seek 10 seconds or change the volume, M to mute, S for shuffle, R for repeat.
- **Click with the keyboard:** T puts a letter on everything you can click. Type it, and it is clicked. Escape hides the letters.
- **Shift + ?** shows them all, in your language.

## Better than the original

They work on every keyboard layout, AZERTY included, never fire while you type in a field, and each group can be turned off in the settings.

## Made to last

It only uses spacetify's API, never Spotify's code: it keeps working after Spotify's updates.
