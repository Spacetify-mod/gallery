# Skip Unplayable

Spotify sometimes stops on a song or a video it cannot play here: not allowed in your country, removed, a broken local file. Skip Unplayable goes on to the next one instead of staying stuck.

## How it works

- When a new track is still at its very beginning a few seconds after it started, while you did not pause it, it is skipped.
- A short message says which one, and why.
- After 3 skips in a row it stops, so a lost connection never empties a whole playlist. Without internet, nothing is skipped.

## Better than the original

Spicetify's *Auto Skip Videos* only skips videos. This one catches anything that cannot play, and never runs away with your playlist.

## Settings

How many seconds to wait before skipping (6 by default), and the message.

## Made to last

It only uses spacetify's API, never Spotify's code: it keeps working after Spotify's updates.
