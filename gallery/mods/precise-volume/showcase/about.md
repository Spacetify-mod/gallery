# Precise Volume

The volume in %, next to the volume bar. Change it with the mouse wheel, or click the number to type the exact value you want.

## Better than Spotify's slider

- Spotify's slider only stops **every 10 %**. With this extension it can be set to **any value**, and dragged finely too.
- The mouse wheel works over the volume bar and over the number.

## Settings

The wheel step (5 % by default), and turning the wheel off.
