# Full Screen

The song playing, full screen: a big, sharp cover, a background blurred from its colours, the title, the artists, the progress bar and the controls.

## How to use it

- Click the **full screen** button next to the volume, or press **F**.
- Leave with **Escape**, the **×** button, or the same button again.
- Drag the progress bar to move in the song.

## Better than the usual full screen

- The cover is taken in **high definition** when Spotify has it.
- The controls are **Spotify's own buttons**: shuffle, previous, play, next and repeat do exactly what they do in the player bar, with the same state.
- It never reaches into Spotify's code, so it keeps working after updates.

## Settings

Blurred background, controls, the **F** key, and hiding the window borders too.
