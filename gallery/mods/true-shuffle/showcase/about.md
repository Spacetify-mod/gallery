# True Shuffle

Spotify's own shuffle prefers some songs. True Shuffle mixes them with the Fisher–Yates method, where every order is equally likely: every song once, none favoured.

## How to use it

- **Right-click** a playlist, an album or your Liked Songs, then **Play with True Shuffle**.
- Or the **True Shuffle** button in the player bar, to mix what is playing now.

## Better than the original

It takes **every** song of a playlist or of your Liked Songs, however many: it was tried with 1 246 songs. When the mixed songs are over, it stops, or lets Spotify go on (in the settings).

## Made to last

It only uses spacetify's API, never Spotify's code: it keeps working after Spotify's updates.
