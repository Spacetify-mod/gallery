# Trash Bin

Throw away songs or artists you never want to hear again: when one of them starts, it is skipped by itself.

## How to use it

- Click the **trash** button next to the volume while a song plays.
- Choose **the song**, or **one of its artists**.
- The same menu lists what is in the trash, with a **Restore** button for each.

## Good to know

- The trash button turns red when the song playing is in the trash.
- A short message says which song was skipped, and why (you can turn it off).
- The trash is kept in Spotify, on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
