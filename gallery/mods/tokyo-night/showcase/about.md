# Tokyo Night

Tokyo Night celebrates the lights of downtown Tokyo at night: deep blues and glowing city colours.

## What it changes

- **Every colour of Spotify**: all 16 of its colour sets, so no Spotify green is left anywhere, not even in success messages, white buttons or menus.
- **The frame too**: the top bar, the player bar and the space between the panels take the theme's darkest colour, where most themes leave them black.
- **The bright blue accent** on the play buttons, the song playing, liked songs and selected text.
- **Always readable**: every text was checked for contrast against its background.

## Built to last

It only uses Spotify's colour variables and its `data-testid` hooks, never generated class names, so it keeps working after Spotify updates. `spacetify doctor` checks it against your Spotify.
