# Bookmarks

Keep a moment of a song (a drop, a verse, a solo) and play it again from that exact second, in one click.

## How to use it

- While a song plays, click the **bookmark** button next to the volume, then **Keep this moment**.
- Click a bookmark to play it again: spacetify opens its album, starts the song and goes to the moment.
- The **×** removes a bookmark.

## Good to know

- It does what you would do yourself, with Spotify's own pages and buttons: nothing reaches into Spotify's code.
- Bookmarks are kept in Spotify, on this computer.
